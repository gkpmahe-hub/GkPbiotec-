Kerala & Puducherry · Marine Biotechnology

From the fishing harbours of Kerala to the collagen matrix.

Thalassa Marine Biosciences converts by-product fish skin and scale from Kerala's coastal fisheries into pharmaceutical-grade collagen peptides, regenerative biomaterials and skin-microbiome research — built on a founder's background in protein purification, structural characterisation and postdoctoral microbiome research.

Why marine, why here

A coastline with 590 km of raw material, and a science team that can characterise every batch.

Kerala's fishing harbours generate large volumes of fish skin, scale and swim-bladder by-product every day — the same tissue that yields type I marine collagen. Most of it is discarded or sold as low-value waste. Thalassa exists to intercept that stream at the harbour, extract and purify the collagen under controlled conditions, and validate its structure and purity the way a pharmaceutical lab would — not the way a commodity supplement plant does.

Product & service lines

Five lines, one raw material, one quality standard.

Nutraceutical

Marine Collagen Peptides + Vitamin C

Low-molecular-weight hydrolysed peptides from Kerala-sourced fish skin, fortified with vitamin C to support the body's own collagen synthesis.

Biomedical

Collagen Sponge

Porous, resorbable collagen scaffold for wound dressing and haemostasis, built from purified type I marine collagen.

Biomedical

Collagen Matrix

Structured 3D scaffold for tissue engineering and regenerative research, characterised for pore architecture and cross-link density.
