Why marine, why now

Under-used coastal by-product, and a science team that can characterise every batch.

Coastal fish processing generates large volumes of skin, scale and swim-bladder by-product — the same tissue that yields type I marine collagen. Most of it is discarded or sold as low-value waste. Volna intercepts that stream, extracts and purifies the collagen under controlled conditions, and validates its structure and purity the way a pharmaceutical lab would — not the way a commodity supplement plant does. Marine is the starting raw material; other collagen sources are on the roadmap as the product line grows.
