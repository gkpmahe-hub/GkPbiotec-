About

Founded on protein science, grown from marine biology.

Dr. G. K. Prasanth

Founder

Dr. Prasanth holds a PhD in Biotechnology, with doctoral research focused on protein isolation, purification and 2D/3D structural characterisation. His postdoctoral work turned to the skin microbiome — its composition, its relationship to collagen biology, and its association with disease.

Volna Biotech is the point where those two research threads meet a practical resource: marine by-product that is currently under-used and under-valued. The company's product line and its microbiome consultation service both trace directly back to that founding expertise — starting with marine collagen, with other collagen sources planned as the line diversifies.

What the company stands for

  • Characterise before you formulate — every batch gets structural data, not just a purity number
  • Traceable sourcing, documented from raw material to finished lot
  • A research-services line that keeps the company close to academic and clinical science
  • Products built to a documentation standard, not just a supplement-market standard

Geography

Kerala or Puducherry for the registered base.

Kerala

An active marine-biotech and seafood-processing ecosystem, coastal access for raw-material sourcing, and proximity to biotechnology research institutions for collaboration.

Puducherry

A compact Union Territory with its own coastline, a research-institute presence, and — depending on current incentive schemes — potential advantages for a small biotech manufacturing unit. Both locations warrant a direct comparison of state biotech-policy incentives, land cost and logistics before final registration.
