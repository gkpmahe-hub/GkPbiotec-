About

Founded on protein science, grown from the coastline.

Dr. G. K. Prasanth

Founder

Dr. Prasanth holds a PhD in Biotechnology, with doctoral research focused on protein isolation, purification and 2D/3D structural characterisation. His postdoctoral work turned to the skin microbiome — its composition, its relationship to collagen biology, and its association with disease.

Thalassa Marine Biosciences is the point where those two research threads meet a practical resource: the volume of fish skin and scale generated daily by Kerala's fishing harbours, most of it currently under-used. The company's product line and its microbiome consultation service both trace directly back to that founding expertise.

What the company stands for

  • Characterise before you formulate — every batch gets structural data, not just a purity number
  • Traceable sourcing from named harbours along the Kerala coastline
  • A research-services line that keeps the company close to academic and clinical science
  • Products built to a documentation standard, not just a supplement-market standard

Geography

Kerala for raw material, Kerala or Puducherry for the registered base.

Kerala

Direct access to fishing harbours along the coastline for raw-material sourcing, an active marine-biotech and seafood-processing ecosystem, and proximity to biotechnology research institutions for collaboration.

Puducherry

A compact Union Territory with its own coastline and fisheries, a research-institute presence, and — depending on current incentive schemes — potential advantages for a small biotech manufacturing unit. Both locations warrant a direct comparison of state biotech-policy incentives, land cost and logistics before final registration.
