Facility

A lab built around one question: what is this protein, exactly?

Five connected zones take raw marine material from the harbour to a characterised, batch-documented ingredient — mirroring the founder's own path through protein purification, structural analysis and microbiome research.

ZONE 01

Raw Material Reception & Cold Chain

Fish skin, scale and swim-bladder by-product intake from partner harbours along the Kerala coast, logged by source and species, and held under controlled cold storage ahead of processing.

walk-in cold room · batch traceability logging · species/source verification bench
ZONE 02

Protein Isolation & Purification Suite

Enzymatic and acid/alkaline extraction lines feeding into chromatographic purification — the core capability from the founder's doctoral training, scaled from bench to pilot volumes.

FPLC / chromatography systems · ultrafiltration & tangential flow filtration · centrifuges · freeze-dryer (lyophiliser)
ZONE 03

Structural Characterisation Lab

2D and 3D structural characterisation of every purified batch: secondary structure, molecular weight distribution, and higher-order assembly, before a lot is released to formulation.

SDS-PAGE / electrophoresis · circular dichroism (CD) spectrometer · FTIR · dynamic light scattering (DLS) · access to SEM/TEM imaging
ZONE 04

Microbiome & Bioinformatics Unit

Sequencing-data analysis and pipeline development for the skin/gut-microbiome consultation service, connecting collagen biology to microbiome composition and disease association.

NGS data pipelines · 16S/shotgun metagenomics analysis · compute workstation cluster · statistical & bioinformatics software stack
ZONE 05

Pilot Formulation & Quality Control

Small-batch formulation of peptide powders, sponges and matrices, paired with a QC bench that checks every release lot against the specification set in Zone 03.

pilot mixing & drying line · sterile prep bench · stability chamber · QC release testing bench

Standards

Documentation the way an academic lab writes it, not the way a commodity plant skips it.

Every collagen lot carries a certificate of analysis covering molecular weight profile, purity, and — where relevant — secondary/tertiary structural data. The intent is to sell an ingredient that a formulator, a hospital, or a research partner can independently verify, not just a powder with a marketing claim on the label.
