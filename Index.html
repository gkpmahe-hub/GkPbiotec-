About Us

BioNutra Innovations Pvt. Ltd. is a proposed biotechnology company dedicated to the research, development and commercialization of evidence-based nutraceuticals. Our goal is to bridge biotechnology research with natural health solutions that promote wellness and improve quality of life.

Proposed Nutraceutical Products

ImmunoBoost+

A botanical formulation enriched with vitamins, minerals and plant-derived bioactive compounds designed to support immune health.

GutBiome Pro

A probiotic and prebiotic formulation intended to promote gut microbiome balance and digestive wellness.

NeuroVital

A nutraceutical blend containing omega fatty acids, antioxidants and herbal extracts for cognitive wellness.

CardioLife

A formulation combining plant sterols, antioxidants and heart-supportive nutrients.

Research & Innovation

Our future research initiatives will focus on molecular biology, functional foods, microbiome science, nutrigenomics, phytochemicals and biotechnology-driven product development.
