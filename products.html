Product & service catalogue

Five lines built on one purified raw material.

Every product below traces back to marine collagen extracted from Kerala coastal by-product and validated in-house for molecular weight, purity and structure before it becomes a formulation.

01 — Nutraceutical

Marine Collagen Peptides, Vitamin C Fortified

What it is

A hydrolysed, low-molecular-weight (<3 kDa) marine collagen peptide powder, enzymatically derived from Kerala-sourced fish skin, fortified with vitamin C to support the body's own collagen synthesis pathway. Neutral taste, high water solubility, suitable for sachets, capsules or beverage powders.

Positioning

  • Skin elasticity, joint comfort and hair/nail nutrition category
  • Fish-sourced — pescatarian-friendly, no bovine/porcine material
  • Batch-level molecular weight and purity documentation
  • Formats: powder sachet, capsule, ready-to-mix jar

02 — Biomedical

Collagen Sponge

What it is

A porous, freeze-dried, resorbable collagen sponge for topical wound care and local haemostasis. Manufactured from purified type I marine collagen, cross-linked to a controlled degradation rate and packaged in sterile-ready formats for surgical and dressing applications.

Positioning

  • Wound-care and surgical haemostat category
  • Pore size and swelling ratio tuned per intended use
  • Route to hospital and veterinary distribution partners
  • Requires biomedical device regulatory pathway before clinical sale

03 — Biomedical / Research

Collagen Matrix

What it is

A structured three-dimensional collagen scaffold for tissue-engineering and cell-culture research, engineered for defined pore architecture, mechanical stiffness and cross-link density. Supplied as sheets, discs or custom-moulded formats for lab and pre-clinical use.

Positioning

  • Sold to research institutions, biomaterial developers, cosmetic-testing labs
  • Each lot shipped with a structural characterisation certificate
  • Custom scaffold geometry and stiffness on request

05 — Services

Consultation & data analysis: skin microbiome, collagen, disease association

What it is

A research-services line drawing directly on postdoctoral work in skin microbiome biology: sequencing-data analysis, bioinformatics pipelines, and consultation for groups studying how the skin (or gut) microbiome relates to collagen turnover and disease phenotypes.

Who it's for

  • Dermatology and wound-care research groups
  • Cosmetic and nutraceutical companies validating product claims
  • Academic labs needing microbiome bioinformatics support
  • Hospitals/clinics studying microbiome–skin disease correlations
